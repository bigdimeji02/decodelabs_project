# E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard

## About the Project

This project looks at e-commerce sales data and turns it into a simple Power BI dashboard.

The goal was to make it easier to see how sales were performing, which products were doing well, where customers came from, and how customers paid for their orders.

The dashboard was built to help a business owner or decision maker understand the data without having to read through a large table of sales records.

## What I Wanted to Find Out

The dashboard was built to answer questions like:

* How much money was made from sales?
* How many orders were made?
* How many units were sold?
* How are sales changing over time?
* Which products bring in the most sales?
* Which products sell the most units?
* What is the average order value for each product?
* Where are customers coming from?
* Which customers have the highest sales value?
* Which payment methods are used most?
* How often are coupon codes used?

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview

The Overview page gives a quick look at the main sales numbers.

It includes:

* Total sales
* Order information
* Total quantity sold
* Sales over time
* Orders over time
* Order status
* Year filters

### 2. Product Performance

This page focuses on product sales.

It shows:

* Sales by product
* Units sold by product
* Average order value by product
* The relationship between units sold and sales
* Total number of products
* Total units sold
* Order status and year filters

### 3. Customer and Marketing

This page looks at customers and how they reached the business.

It shows:

* Sales by referral source
* Sales by customer
* Orders using coupon codes
* Orders by payment method
* Total customers
* Coupon usage percentage
* Order status and year filters

### 4. Insights

The Insights page is used to present the main findings from the analysis in a simple way.

## Tools Used

* **Power BI** – for data analysis and dashboard design
* **DAX** – for calculated measures used in the dashboard
* **Data visualization** – to turn the sales data into easy-to-read charts

## Main Measures

The dashboard includes measures for areas such as:

* Total Products
* Total Units Sold
* Total Customers
* Average Order Value
* Coupon Usage %
* Total Sales
* Order Count

## Charts Used

Different charts were used based on the question being answered.

Examples include:

* Cards for key numbers
* Line charts for changes over time
* Bar and column charts for product and customer comparisons
* Donut charts for order status, coupon use and payment methods
* Scatter charts to compare units sold with sales
* Slicers to filter the dashboard by year and order status

## Key Takeaway

The main purpose of this dashboard is to turn raw e-commerce sales data into information that can support better business decisions.

Instead of looking through rows of sales data, a user can quickly see the main numbers, compare products, understand customer activity and review sales trends.
